Reminder SaaS – Product Requirements
1. Product Vision
Reminder SaaS is an AI-powered scheduling assistant designed for German expats.
The app helps users:
Manage reminders and schedules
Scan government letters
Translate documents
Extract appointment intent
Automatically suggest calendar entries
Receive AI-powered monthly summaries
The app must:
Be beautiful and intuitive
Support Android, iOS, Web
Support biometric login
Support voice commands
Protect user privacy strictly
2. Target Users
Primary:
German expats managing:
Tax deadlines
Health appointments
School events
Government letters
Secondary:
Future multilingual users.
3. Core Features
A. Monthly AI Summary (Default Screen)
AI summary of current month
Category breakdown
Urgent items
Suggested preparations
B. Calendar View
Monthly grid
Add/Edit/Delete schedule
Category color coding
C. Categorized View
Group by:
Health
Tax
School
Personal
Upcoming + overdue indicators
D. Camera → Document Intelligence
Scan letter
Extract text
Detect intent
Translate to English
Suggest calendar entry
Ask confirmation
DO NOT store document
E. Speaker → Voice Commands
Add appointment
Modify schedule
Delete reminder
Add checklist items
4. Privacy Requirements
No document content stored
No personal letter text persisted
Only structured reminder data stored
AI input must be sanitized
